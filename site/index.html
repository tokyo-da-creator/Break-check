                        How prices work

                        Market price
                        What the card or product has actually been selling for on TCGplayer (raw, near mint for cards). Pulled live when you pick something, refreshed every 10 minutes, with the daily price as a fallback.
                        No sales yet
                        The lowest listing is used until it sells.
                        Box spots
                        The box price split evenly across its real pack count (36 for an English Pokémon box, 30 Japanese, 24 One Piece).
                        Verified
                        Every value came from live market data. Bulk, graded values and anything typed by hand are self-reported.
                        Chinese cards
                        Completed-sale prices from CardOS (eBay, TCGplayer and auction houses), fetched when you pick a card.

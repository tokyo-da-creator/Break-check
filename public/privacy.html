Privacy

Last updated 6 October 2026

Break Check is built to work without accounts. Here is everything it does with information.

What stays on your device

Your searches, prices you enter, saved breaks and settings (currency, language) are stored in your own browser. They are not sent to us and we can't see them. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Share links

When you tap Share link, the card image and a short caption you chose to share are uploaded so the link works for others. Shared links last up to one year. Don't include anything you wouldn't post publicly.

Analytics

We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across sites and doesn't collect personal information.

Prices and images

Prices and product images are loaded from TCGplayer (through public price data) and, for Chinese products, PriceCharting. Those requests contain only the product being looked up.

Hosting

The site runs on Cloudflare, which processes standard request data (such as IP address) to deliver and protect the site.

Contact

Questions, corrections or a request to remove a shared link: devxxhector@gmail.com

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
